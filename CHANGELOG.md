# What changed

## 1.1 — 2026-11-21

**Groups**
- Create a group in two steps (pick people, then name, picture and description). Up to 50 people.
- Admins can add and remove people and make other admins. Removing someone changes the group's key.
- Group rules: who may change the name and picture, and who may send (an "admins only" announcement group).
- Join by link: anyone with the link can ask, and an admin has to say yes.
- Messages reach every member over the mesh or the internet, and a member who was offline catches up later.
- Message info lists who **read**, who **got** it, who **has not got it yet** and who **has not joined yet**.
- `@mentions`, "is typing…" in groups, and group history lines ("Maya added Rahul").
- A strip of shared photos in the group info, and a Media screen with tabs for photos, files and links.

**Pinned messages**
- Pin up to 3 messages in a group or a one-to-one chat. A new pin never removes the older ones.

**Calls**
- A **Minimise** button, and a green bar at the top that takes you back to the call.
- Calls show the person's name, never their ID.

**Mesh and Wi-Fi**
- The mesh is **off until you turn it on**. While it is off Pulse never touches Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and opening the app never turns them on.
- Turning Wi-Fi off turns the mesh switch off. A new setting, **Mesh on by default**, turns the mesh on whenever Wi-Fi is on (off unless you choose it).
- The permanent "running in the background" notification is gone. A short notice shows only during a call or for a few seconds while the mesh starts.

**Links**
- Share links are now web addresses (`https://busyboyrahul.github.io/Pulse/…`), so WhatsApp and other apps make them tappable. The page opens Pulse or offers the download. Old `pulse://` links still work.

**Other**
- Chat lock and app lock are separate. Locked chats have a hidden folder.
- Unique `@usernames` with no server (the oldest claim wins).
- Wallpaper opacity and blur sliders; the photo permission is asked once.
- Long-press selects a message and lets you pick more.
- The download is now a GitHub Release instead of a file inside the repository.

## 1.0 — 2026-10-30

First public build: nearby mesh chat with relaying, SOS, calls over the mesh, notifications, profiles, files and voice notes, backup and locks.
